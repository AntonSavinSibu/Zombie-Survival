Endless Waves

Face increasingly deadly hordes. Named bosses appear on special waves, including a full gauntlet at Wave 100.

Deep Upgrade System

Choose from weighted rarity cards every level-up. Crit, pierce, shrapnel, lifesteal, armor, freeze and more.

🔫

Weapon Evolution

Evolve from pistol all the way to Plasma Rifle. Unlock orbit weapons that grow from knives to auto-guns.

🧬

11 Unique Classes

From balanced Survivor to overpowered Executor. Each class has distinct abilities and playstyles.

Local Multiplayer

Create or join rooms with a 6-character code. Play with 2–4 friends on Normal, Hardcore or Nightmare.

Unlock class skins and gun skins with coins and gems. Account system with local saves and leaderboards.
